# The Habit Story: Ambient State Deserves a Reflex Too

## The Property That Was Already There

^origin

My daily notes carry a small piece of frontmatter: `cannabis: true/false`. A single bit that says *today, I partook*. It sits beside `water`, waiting to count ounces toward a daily goal.

These aren't captures in the way [[the_pour_manifesto|the manifesto]] means them. A coffee has a bean, a ratio, a taste worth three paragraphs. A `true` has nothing to say beyond itself. It is **ambient state** — the background hum of a day, not an event in it.

And yet logging that one bit costs more than logging the coffee: open Obsidian, find today's note, click into the properties, flip the value. The manifesto's founding tragedy — *if the process of logging a thought takes longer than the thought itself, the thought dies* — applies with extra irony here, because the "thought" is one bit. So the bit goes unflipped, and the record quietly lies.

`pour habit water 16` — done before the glass is down. That's the whole story.

## Two Shapes of Memory

^creed

Building this forced a line we should have drawn long ago, now inscribed in [[pour-habit-capture|the spec]] as a creed:

> [!quote] Ambient state gets a property. Novel experience gets a note. Never both for the same signal.

Coffee gets an event note because every cup is different. Cannabis gets a property because no occurrence differs from the last in any way worth writing down. And a signal that *could* be derived from event notes must never also be mirrored into a property — two sources of truth drift by week one, and then neither is a memory, just competing guesses.

Pour doesn't gain a "habit tracker." It gains the ability to **reach into a note that already exists and adjust one small true thing about today** — and that primitive belongs to every module, not just this one.

## The Tool That Says "Enough"

^limits

Goals count up: `64/96 oz`, keep going. But the property that started this story wanted the opposite — a **limit**. Not a streak, not a badge. Just a quiet, factual voice at the moment of logging:

> already partaken enough this week (3) — come back Fri, Aug 8.

A goal cheers; a limit steadies. Both are the same gesture — pour telling you where you stand *in the moment you act*, instead of a dashboard telling you on Sunday what you'd rather have known on Tuesday. The past stays Obsidian's to synthesize; the present moment is pour's to serve.
